This is a sample of a Customer Journey Map.

For an interactive webpage, please visit here: https://melissamcgowan.github.io/Customer-Journey-Map/

For a PDF version, please click here: https://github.com/melissamcgowan/Customer-Journey-Map/blob/main/Customer_Success_Journey_Map.pdf

This is designed so that the key motions your sales and CS teams need to take when working with their customers are called out. Each section
has its own theme, like Onboarding, Adoption, and Expansion. Within each section, there are sub-activities or motions that need to be completed. 
Within each sub-activity, the specifics of who, what, when, where, and why are established. Eventually, each of these sub-activities would be
expanded out into its own detailed playbook that dives into the specifics of how that activity is completed, including any tools or assets 
that may be needed. 

*Please note that this is just a sample and is in no way fully complete but serves to give an idea of what's possible.
